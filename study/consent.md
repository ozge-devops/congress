# Informed consent — VESTA briefing study

You are invited to a 45-minute study of how retail investors use Borsa Istanbul
briefings under time pressure. You will see eight historical market snapshots
and decide whether the index or stock is more likely to rise or fall over the
following week.

- You may stop at any time.
- We record your choices, timestamps, NASA-TLX ratings, and a trust item.
- We do not record account numbers or personally identifying portfolio data.
- Scenarios are public historical data; this is not investment advice.
- Results may appear in an academic paper in aggregated form.

By continuing you confirm that you are 18 or older and hold (or have held) a
Turkish retail brokerage account.
