\begin{abstract}
In high-volatility emerging markets such as Borsa Istanbul (BIST), retail investors face a low signal-to-noise ratio created by the divergence between unstructured disclosures and rapidly changing charts. Existing decision-support systems process news and technical pictures independently, and they ignore the investor's \emph{temporal bandwidth}: the same briefing is delivered whether the user has one minute or ten. This paper presents VESTA, a three-layer agentic system that (i)~retrieves portfolio-conditioned textual evidence, (ii)~reads candlestick images and the underlying OHLCV numbers, and (iii)~spends a declared 1-, 3-, or 10-minute budget by changing the depth of the agentic chain. We do \emph{not} treat the scalar cross-modal gate as a methodological contribution; it is a standard sigmoid mixer, compared against GMU and tensor fusion. A previous evaluation protocol that labelled ``anomalies'' from realized volatility is shown to be invalid: a closed-form rule on the tabular OHLCV vector scores 100\%. On a leakage-free next-day direction task over public BIST100 data (2018--2026), no fusion method significantly outperforms GMU (McNemar $p>0.46$ on seed 0). What \emph{does} vary across tiers is coverage, declared latency, and length-normalized information noise. A paper-trading overlay on seed-0 calls does not beat buy-and-hold in the 2025--2026 test window. Source: \url{https://github.com/ozge-devops/congress}.

\section{Introduction}
The financial information landscape is high-entropy. Retail investors sit between the textual density of the Public Disclosure Platform (KAP)~\cite{kap2026} and the visual flux of technical charts. The difficulty is not only volume but \emph{modality mismatch}: a constructive disclosure can coincide with a failed breakout, and the two streams must be reconciled before a decision. Conventional DSS pipelines keep NLP and computer vision in semantic silos. A second, less discussed failure mode is temporal: systems emit the same information density whether the user has sixty seconds or ten minutes~\cite{eppler2004overload,miller1956magical,sweller1988cognitive}. On a mobile-first retail platform this mismatch is not cosmetic; it is extra intrinsic load~\cite{sweller2023clt}.

Recent LLM trading agents attack related problems from a different angle. FinMem layers memory by information half-life~\cite{yu2024finmem}. Neither that line of work nor typical chart-reading trading bots treat the \emph{user's available time} as a first-class computational budget, and they are evaluated as trading agents rather than as a retail decision-support interface. VESTA occupies that gap.

\paragraph{Contributions.}
This revision is explicit about what is, and is not, claimed.
\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{Time-budgeted orchestration.} A Temporal Orchestration Layer maps a 1-, 3-, or 10-minute user budget onto agent-chain depth. We report per-tier coverage, a declared latency budget, token load and length-normalized noise---quantities previous drafts described only in prose.
\item \textbf{A leakage-aware evaluation protocol} for multimodal BIST decision support. We show that a realized-volatility ``anomaly'' label is a closed-form function of the \emph{tabular} OHLCV vector at day $t$, and we replace it with a forward-looking directional task. The public chart is bars $t-40,\ldots,t-1$ and does not contain today's candle. A tabular OHLCV baseline is reported whenever chart images are used~\cite{jiang2023reimagining}.
\item \textbf{External fusion baselines and a decision-quality backtest.} Scalar gating is compared with GMU~\cite{arevalo2017gmu} and tensor fusion~\cite{zadeh2017tfn}. Acting on system output is evaluated as a long/short overlay, not only as classification F1.
\end{enumerate}
What we withdraw: Cross-Modal Gated Fusion is a standard scalar sigmoid over concatenated features~\cite{arevalo2017gmu,jiang2022cmga}. It is an implementation choice, not a contribution. We also withdraw the previously reported 92.4\% anomaly F1; that figure scored a vision encoder on a label that is a closed-form function of the tabular vector, not anomaly detection.

\section{Related Work}
\subsection{LLMs and agentic workflows in finance}
Financial NLP has moved from sentiment scoring~\cite{sohangir2018big} to domain LLMs such as FinGPT~\cite{yang2023fingpt}. Hallucination on prices and terminology remains a documented failure mode~\cite{kang2023deficiency}, which is why VESTA grounds text in retrieval~\cite{lewis2020rag} rather than in unaugmented generation. Tool-using agents~\cite{yao2023react} and trading-oriented systems with layered memory~\cite{yu2024finmem} are the closest architectural relatives. FinMem allocates memory by the \emph{staleness of facts}; VESTA allocates compute by the \emph{staleness of the user's attention}. FinMem-style agents optimize for the trader; VESTA is scored as a briefing engine for a time-constrained retail user.

\subsection{Multimodal fusion and chart understanding}
GMU learns a multiplicative gate between modalities~\cite{arevalo2017gmu}; Tensor Fusion Networks model unimodal and bimodal interactions via an outer product~\cite{zadeh2017tfn}; MulT uses directional pairwise cross-modal attention~\cite{tsai2019mult}. Jiang and Ji add a forget-gate over cross-modal attention maps~\cite{jiang2022cmga}. VESTA's mixer is the bimodal special case of this family; we therefore treat GMU and TFN as the mandatory external controls.

On the vision side, Pix2Struct and MatCha pretrain screenshot-to-text models that read plots~\cite{lee2023pix2struct,liu2023matcha}. In asset pricing, Jiang, Kelly and Xiu show that CNNs on price-trend \emph{images} extract return-predictive patterns~\cite{jiang2023reimagining}. That result is double-edged for our setting: it motivates VisualClaw, and it obliges a baseline that sees the same series as numbers. Without the tabular arm, a vision score on a label computed from OHLCV is uninterpretable.

BIST100 forecasting from technical indicators is studied by Akturk et al.~\cite{akturk2026bist}; their features are numeric, which is the required control whenever a chart encoder is scored.

\subsection{Cognitive load and adaptive interfaces}
Cognitive load theory requires that presented density match working-memory capacity~\cite{sweller1988cognitive,sweller2023clt,miller1956magical}. Information overload is a long-standing MIS construct~\cite{eppler2004overload}. Adaptive GUIs have a documented evaluation discipline: measure whether adaptation helps the task, not only whether the widget changed~\cite{gajos2006exploring,findlater2009adaptive}. Summarization evaluation that ignores output length systematically rewards brevity~\cite{sun2019compare}. These four literatures jointly constrain our metrics (Sect.~\ref{sec:metrics}).

\subsection{DataClaw0: portfolio-conditioned retrieval}
DataClaw0 is an agentic RAG loop~\cite{lewis2020rag,yao2023react}, not a summarizer. Documents $N$ and the user portfolio $P$ are embedded with M3-Embedding~\cite{chen2024m3}. A lightweight sector-to-macro graph scores sensitivity to gold, USD/TRY and tourism. Relevance of document $d_i$ is
\begin{equation}
R(d_i)=\sigma\!\left(\alpha\cos(v_{d_i},v_P)+\sum_{j\in\{G,X,T\}}\beta_j\Delta_j\right),
\label{eq:rel}
\end{equation}
where $\Delta_j$ is the percentage change of macro-indicator $j$ and $\sigma$ is the logistic function. The fusion tables below instantiate the textual stream as an eight-dimensional macro brief concatenated with eight KAP daily features (filing counts, lexicon scores, earnings/dividend/buyback flags, signed KAP polarity). Production DataClaw0 uses Eq.~\eqref{eq:rel} over live KAP bodies and M3 embeddings. The public stand-in is MiniLM~\cite{reimers2019sentence} and public BGE-M3 over KAP concatenations---not Infina HTML.

\subsection{VisualClaw, and the numbers behind the picture}
VisualClaw renders an OHLC window as an image. Production uses a compact vision backbone in the spirit of ViT~\cite{dosovitskiy2021vit} with recency-weighted position
\begin{equation}
E_{\mathrm{total}}=E_{\mathrm{patch}}+E_{\mathrm{pos}}\cdot\bigl(1+\lambda\, t/T\bigr).
\label{eq:pos}
\end{equation}
Public tables do not train Eq.~\eqref{eq:pos}: they use a $24\times24$ grayscale MLP and a frozen ImageNet ViT-B/16 CLS probe. The chart is bars $t-40,\ldots,t-1$; tabular features are computed at $t$. The numeric control remains mandatory~\cite{jiang2023reimagining}.

\subsection{Fusion, used not proposed}
\label{sec:fusion}
Let $H_{\mathrm{text}}$ and $H_{\mathrm{vision}}$ be the two streams. The scalar gate used in the production stack is
\begin{align}
g&=\sigma\bigl(W_g[H_{\mathrm{text}};H_{\mathrm{vision}}]+b_g\bigr),\label{eq:gate}\\
H_{\mathrm{fused}}&=g\cdot\tanh(H_{\mathrm{text}})+(1-g)\cdot\tanh(H_{\mathrm{vision}}).\label{eq:fuse}
\end{align}
Equations~\eqref{eq:gate}--\eqref{eq:fuse} are the production form (learned $W_g$). Public CPU mixers fuse two-dimensional class scores: a fixed score-gap gate, score-space GMU/TFN, and one frozen random-projection MulT-style layer, each followed by a sklearn MLP. No mixer is advertised as novel.

\subsection{Temporal Orchestration Layer}
\label{sec:tol}
Given fused confidence $|g-0.5|$ and a user budget $B\in\{1,3,10\}$ minutes, the layer selects a chain:
\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{T1 (flash).} Emit a gated alert only if $|g-0.5|>\tau$; otherwise abstain. Target latency $<1$\,s.
\item \textbf{T3 (context).} Always emit the fused call plus one retrieval hop over historical analogues.
\item \textbf{T10 (briefing).} Expand into a macro explanation and a hedge sketch (e.g.\ airline exposure versus a gold certificate), at several seconds of wall-clock time.
\end{itemize}
Depth, not the mixer, is what changes with $B$. Section~\ref{sec:tiers} reports coverage, latency, tokens and noise per tier.

\section{Evaluation Protocol}
\label{sec:protocol}
\subsection{Why the previous headline task does not measure anomalies}
\label{sec:leak}
A previous draft defined a $2\sigma$ realized-vol ``anomaly'' and reported 92.4\% F1. Here the flag is 20-day vol above the mean $+2\sigma$ of the previous 60 vol observations---a closed-form function of the \emph{tabular} vector at $t$ (100\% F1). The public chart does not contain today's candle, so vision F1 on this label is lag-1 recovery, not anomaly detection. We keep the task only as a \emph{diagnostic}.

\subsection{Primary task: forward direction}
The primary label at close of day $t$ is $y_{t+1}=\mathbb{1}[r_{t+1}>0]$, the sign of the next session's BIST100 simple return. This quantity is not a function of the information set at $t$. We also report a five-session horizon. Splits are chronological (70/15/15) to block look-ahead~\cite{akturk2026bist}.

\label{sec:metrics}

\textbf{Information noise, revised.} Let $N_{\mathrm{raw}}$ be tokens in the unprocessed window and $N_{\mathrm{del}}$ tokens actually delivered. Let $N_{\mathrm{irrel}}$ be delivered tokens that are not in a relevance lexicon for that window. The previous index $\mathrm{IN}_{\mathrm{old}}=N_{\mathrm{irrel}}/N_{\mathrm{raw}}$ rewards short answers: a 40-token briefing that is 80\% filler still looks excellent against a 700-token feed~\cite{sun2019compare}. We therefore report
\begin{equation}
\mathrm{IN}_{\mathrm{new}}=N_{\mathrm{irrel}}/N_{\mathrm{del}},
\label{eq:innew}
\end{equation}
i.e.\ the fraction of the \emph{payload} that is non-actionable, together with the compression ratio $N_{\mathrm{del}}/N_{\mathrm{raw}}$. $\mathrm{IN}_{\mathrm{old}}$ is retained only to show the bias. In the public runtime both counts are computed on \emph{templated} token bags with filler padding, not on production LLM tokens.

\textbf{Decision quality.} Classification F1 does not say whether a user who \emph{acts} is better off~\cite{findlater2009adaptive}. We paper-trade a long/short overlay: $+1$ if the model predicts up, $-1$ otherwise, with 5\,bp round-trip cost, and compare Sharpe, total return and max drawdown to buy-and-hold. This is not a substitute for a human-subjects study (Sect.~\ref{sec:user}); it is the missing computational control.

\subsection{Public replication corpus}
Production KAP HTML bodies and M3 embeddings cannot leave Infina. Numbers below use \textbf{VESTA-Public}: XU100.IS, USDTRY and COMEX gold from 24 May 2018 to 19 August 2026 (2{,}052 index windows after a 40-day lookback; test $n=308$ from 27 May 2025), 27 BIST names, and 39{,}890 public KAP list filings. Tables~\ref{tab:leak}--\ref{tab:bt} are index-level; the 37{,}046-row event panel also labels constituents. KAP-only polarity: 7{,}172 / 3{,}742 / 26{,}132 (bullish / bearish / neutral); 15{,}212 rows have a cached HTML body. Mixers use the 16-d macro+KAP vector; MiniLM and public BGE-M3 are unimodal stand-ins. We do not recycle private VESTA-10K F1.

\section{Experiments}
\label{sec:exp}
Unimodal encoders are sklearn MLPs (64-32, Adam, early stopping) on standardized features. Vision sees a $24\times24$ grayscale candlestick of bars $t-40,\ldots,t-1$; tabular sees eleven OHLCV/macro features at $t$, including the ingredients of the leaked vol rule; text sees the sixteen-dimensional macro+KAP vector. Five seeds $\{0,\ldots,4\}$. This is a CPU-reproducible instantiation, not a 70B distillation. Public BGE-M3 and frozen ViT-B/16 are unimodal probes; they are not plugged into the mixers in Table~\ref{tab:fwd}.

\subsection{Diagnostic: the leaked volatility label}
Table~\ref{tab:leak} and Fig.~\ref{fig:leak} confirm the closed-form critique. The $2\sigma$ rule on the tabular vector scores 100\% F1. A tabular MLP that is given those numbers reaches 96.8\% accuracy (macro-F1 86.6\%; the F1 drop is class imbalance: 9.4\% positives). The $24\times24$ vision encoder, which never sees day $t$'s candle, falls to 90.6\% accuracy and 58.2\% F1.

\section{User-study protocol (not yet run)}
\label{sec:user}
A computational backtest still does not measure cognitive effort~\cite{gajos2006exploring,findlater2009adaptive}. We specify, but do not fake, a 12-participant study. Raw session-sign hits $4/8$ name-level week golds. Index mixers are scored only on the two XU100 scenarios (text $0/2$, gated $1/2$). NASA-TLX is blank.

\section{Discussion and limitations}
The scientific contribution that survives this revision is the \emph{problem formulation and the evaluation}: time as a budget, fusion as a swap-in block, leakage made visible, and actionability measured. The engineering stack (DataClaw0, VisualClaw, gated delivery) is a coherent system, not a new learning rule. Weaker directional numbers on a public daily corpus are preferable to a 92\% F1 that does not mean what it says.

Limitations: public BGE-M3 is list-text, not Infina HTML; frozen ViT-B/16 does not beat the $24\times24$ MLP; DePlot/MatCha are zero-shot on 308 days; twelve participants have not been recruited; the public chart is $t-40,\ldots,t-1$; $\mathrm{IN}$ and tier latencies are simulated; Table~\ref{tab:fwd} mixers fuse unimodal scores, not learned $W_g$.

\section{Conclusion}
VESTA is a time-budgeted multimodal briefing system for BIST retail users. After correcting the evaluation, three statements hold. First, a volatility-threshold label is not an anomaly task; a closed-form numeric baseline scores perfectly. Second, on next-day direction, standard mixers including GMU are statistically indistinguishable from the scalar gate we used to ship. Third, the Temporal Orchestration Layer \emph{does} change measurable quantities---coverage, latency, and length-normalized noise---even when it does not yet change alpha. Those are the claims we are willing to take to another venue.
